## Current Focus

**Project 002: Measuring and Communicating the Realized Business Value of Cybersecurity**

- Slug: `project-002-cyber-value`
- Status: active (literature review stage)
- Scope: role-neutral. No specific security role is assumed or tested.
- Method: Design Science Research, evaluated without participants. No data collected from people. Framework v0.6 in `research/project-002-cyber-value/framework/`. AI use log in `research/project-002-cyber-value/ai-use-log.md`.
- Research questions (framework v0.6, Section 2):
  - RQ1: What does published evidence say makes a cybersecurity value claim credible to business executives?
  - RQ2: What lightweight, repeatable mechanism can capture and communicate realized value without adding governance overhead?
  - RQ3: What capabilities do validation and communication of security value require, based on published evidence? (exploratory)
- Design propositions (argued from literature and public evidence): P1 (value is realized only when the beneficiary confirms it), P2 (the binding constraint is translation and validation, not measurement technique), and P3 (realized value reporting complements protection-level governance such as outcome-driven metrics and protection-level agreements; to be tested).
- Source materials: `research/project-002-cyber-value/` in the private `joechawley-research-sources` repo (not in the site repo)
